# Historical replication cases

These selected cases informed the skill before this release candidate. They used earlier instruction versions and paper-specific approvals. Their results are retained as historical evidence, not rerun results for `0.1.0-beta.1`. This table is not an aggregate success-rate estimate.

| Case | Route and finding | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thompson et al. (2020), [VBM package](https://github.com/andybhall/vbm-replication-extension) | Author-package benchmark. Tables 2 and 3 were reproduced; the initial comparison found a maximum difference of 0.0005 across 12 core coefficients. | Evidence for the tested package and tables. It does not establish arbitrary-paper coverage. |
| Lepage (2024), *Experience-Based Discrimination* | Independent R reconstruction. The historical ledger has 190 direct comparisons, 161 raw passes and 29 retained residuals; five figure routes passed their declared contracts. | Forty-three open-specification items and one unexecuted appendix simulation remain. Strong core experimental evidence with partial appendix coverage; its historical `pass` label is not recoded as a new-release test result. |
| [Vulnerability and Clientelism](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190565) | Independent R reconstruction; CP7-approved partial replication. Of 787 primary targets, 471 were compared: 436 strict passes and 35 residuals. | 268 not comparable, 36 not closed and 12 blocked. Prepared-data boundaries, inference and figure limits remain. |
| [Land Rental Markets](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20221234) | Independent R reconstruction; CP7-approved partial replication. Of 4,564 primary targets, 2,038 were compared: 1,334 strict passes, 42 passes with notes and 662 residuals. | 2,493 not closed and 33 blocked. Group definitions, follow-up data and specialized methods remain unresolved. |
| [Zero-Sum Thinking](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20240692) | Independent R reconstruction; CP7-approved partial replication. Of 4,341 primary targets, 2,637 were compared: 1,234 strict passes and 1,403 residuals. | 550 not comparable, 170 not closed and 984 blocked. Source linkage, historical geography, inference and figure decisions remain open. |

Historical reports and the project's retained closure records support these summaries. Full reports, private review transcripts and original datasets are not bundled here. Paper links identify the studies; they do not provide the project's audit evidence. Public distribution of individual evidence packages needs a separate review of permissions and contents.

The recent cases tested source recovery, missing-code reconstruction, sample lineage, numerical eligibility and bounded repairs. They also exposed repeated problems: usable files with incomplete semantic definitions, documented methods with missing operational choices, and figures whose construction checks did not establish agreement with the paper. The current instructions retain those distinctions.

For the tests performed on the release package itself, see [release checks](release-checks.md).
